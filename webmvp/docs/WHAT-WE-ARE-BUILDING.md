# What We Are Building

A simple web page to prove our chord app idea works.

## In plain English

1. You click **Import Song** and pick **Twinkle Twinkle Little Star**
2. The lyrics appear — you type basic chords above each part (`C`, `F`, `G`)
3. The app figures out the numbers automatically (`I`, `IV`, `V`)
4. You hit **Save** — the song stays in the browser even after you close the tab
5. Anytime you open Twinkle Twinkle, you pick a new key and all chords change instantly

That's the whole prototype.

## Why

Before we build a database, admin panel, and mobile app — we need to know: **does the transpose logic actually work?**

## What success looks like

Enter Twinkle in key C as `C C F C` / `F C G C`. Switch to G. See `G G C G` / `C G D G`. Done.
